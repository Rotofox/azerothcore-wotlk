# Ratified decisions (carry verbatim)

1. **Stay-at-base outcome — ADD IT.** The quality roll now includes an explicit STAY outcome: a STAY roll leaves the item at its base quality (no variant swap; the enchant roll still runs on the base item). Every rate row includes a STAY weight. Rationale (engineer): without it every eligible item always upgrades and an endgame epic always becomes legendary.
2. **Table shape — per-base-quality rows.** `mod_re_rates` is keyed by (ilvl band, base quality) — separate weight rows for common/uncommon/rare/epic bases per ilvl band, columns STAY + the tiers available ABOVE that base (no-backward rule). 4 base qualities × 7 ilvl bands = 28 rows. No more single-table normalization.
3. **Upgrade-frequency targets (engineer's words):** roughly 2 in 5 low-level drops upgrade; 1 in 4 endgame drops. Concretely: low ilvl (1–70) STAY ≈ 50–60 %, mid (71–180) STAY ≈ 60–70 %, endgame (181+) STAY ≈ 72–78 %.
4. **Legendary tail — GENEROUS (engineer's words: "5%+ (generous)"):** blended target ≈ 1 in 20 eligible drops at endgame (~5 %), ≈ 1 in 10 at low ilvl. T4/T5 are the legendary rolls.
5. **Epic-base consequence (engineer's tension, resolved with a recommended default):** for epic bases the only variants are legendary (T4/T5), so an epic that upgrades IS a legendary. To keep the BLENDED legendary rate near the 1-in-20 target (rather than every ~4th epic drop becoming legendary), the epic rows carry a high STAY weight — recommended default epic STAY ≈ 93 % at endgame, tapering to ≈ 80 % at low ilvl. Flag this number prominently in §4.3 with the one-line rationale so the engineer can tune it deliberately.
6. **Legendary bases — REVERT to the engineer's rule:** legendary items get NO variants. Undo §1.4's "legendary items need variants to make them stronger" change; legendary bases roll enchants only (unchanged from the current module).
